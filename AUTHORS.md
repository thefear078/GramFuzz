## Contributing

GramFuzz is currently maintained as a solo project. If you wish to contribute:

1. Open an issue to discuss the proposed change
2. Ensure your contribution aligns with the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
3. Do not include AI-generated commits attributed to third-party tooling in the author field

Pull requests from unauthorized security researchers testing third-party systems will not be accepted.
