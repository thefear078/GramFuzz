## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the GramFuzz project a respectful, inclusive, and ethically responsible experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community and the security field
- Show empathy toward other community members
- Use GramFuzz **only** for authorized security testing
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly via Telegram **@thefear007**

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Using GramFuzz or contributing to it for **unauthorized** offensive security activities
- Sharing exploit techniques publicly before coordinated disclosure
- Doxxing, stalking, or threatening any individual

---

## Ethical Security Research

All contributors and users must ensure their work supports **authorized security testing only**.

- Do not add features designed primarily to facilitate unauthorized access
- Do not provide support for attacks against systems without permission
- Document the intended authorized use case for new modules or features
- Follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability disclosure

GramFuzz exists to help defenders secure Telegram applications — not to enable abuse.

---

## Enforcement

The project maintainer ([@kiurakku](https://github.com/kiurakku)) may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported via Telegram: **[@thefear007](https://t.me/thefear007)**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and safety of reporters.
